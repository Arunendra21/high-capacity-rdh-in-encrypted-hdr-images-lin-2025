# Processing Notes — High-Capacity RDH in Encrypted HDR Images (Lin 2025)

- **Paper:** Alfrindo Lin, Yun-Ting Lin, Wen-Ting Jao, Yuan-Yu Tsai, Pattern Analysis and Applications, vol. 28, 2025
- **Reproduction tier:** C
- **Status:** Completed (literature review + partial demo)

## Reproduction scope
**Reproduced:** multi-MSB / leading-zero predictability on a synthetic 16-bit HDR image, giving several bpp of embedding room. **Not reproduced:** real HDR formats, the encryption, and the multi-participant label-map sharing.

## Why full reproduction is not feasible here
No real HDR datasets/formats or the sharing protocol are available in the workspace.

## Honesty note
Demo numbers are from the included code on synthetic HDR; paper figures are not independently reproduced.
